What is Anki?

Anki is a free and open-source flashcard program that uses a Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler (FSRS) for optimal card review timing and faster progress.

Anki uses decks to organize flashcards into specific categories, allowing users to efficiently study topics by reviewing cards in those decks.

You can make your own decks or use Community made decks a good and popular starter deck is Kaishi 1.5k other decks can be found under ankiweb.net/shared/decks.
